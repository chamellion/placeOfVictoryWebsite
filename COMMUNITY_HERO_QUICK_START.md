## 🎨 Design Highlights

### Colors
- **Hero**: Primary blue gradient overlay
- **Donate Button**: Yellow (high urgency)
- **Impact Button**: Primary blue (trust)
- **Volunteer Button**: White outline (clean)
- **Metrics**: Individual colors per category

### Animations
- **Hero**: Fade in, slide up, zoom in
- **Counters**: Count from 0 to target in 2 seconds
- **Services**: Staggered fade (150ms delays)
- **Hovers**: Lift, scale, rotate, arrow slide
